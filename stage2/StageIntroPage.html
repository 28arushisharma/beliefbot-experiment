Stage 2 — Sequential Information Acquisition

As in Stage 1, you will try to identify which jar balls are drawn from. The two possible jars are:

  • Red Jar: 14 red balls, 6 blue balls
  • Blue Jar: 14 blue balls, 6 red balls

You will play {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} rounds in total, split into two sets of {{ C.ROUNDS_PER_JAR }} rounds:

  • Rounds 1–{{ C.ROUNDS_PER_JAR }}: all draws come from the same jar (Jar 1).
  • Rounds {{ rounds_per_jar_plus_1 }}–{{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }}: all draws come from a new jar (Jar 2), which may be different from Jar 1.

The jar resets to 20 balls at the start of each round. You will not be told which jar it is — use the draws to figure it out.

How each round works
  1. You are shown {{ C.N_INITIAL_DRAWS }} balls drawn without replacement from the jar.
  2. You may then purchase additional balls, one at a time, at ${{ draw_cost }} each. Up to {{ C.MAX_ADDITIONAL }} additional balls are available ({{ C.N_INITIAL_DRAWS }} initial + {{ C.MAX_ADDITIONAL }} extra = {{ max_balls_total }} balls total maximum).
  3. After seeing as many balls as you want, you guess Red Jar or Blue Jar.
  4. Correct guess: ${{ C.PAYOFF_CORRECT }}. Wrong guess: $0. Your net earnings = payoff − cost of extra balls purchased.
Note: If you buy many extra balls and guess wrong, your net earnings for that round can be negative. Think carefully about whether the additional information is worth the cost.
