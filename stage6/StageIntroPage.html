Welcome to Stage 6 — Your role: Writer

You begin this stage as the Writer. In later matches you may be assigned the Reader role instead; you will be told if your role changes. Each round, balls are drawn from either a Red Jar (14 red, 6 blue) or a Blue Jar (14 blue, 6 red) containing 20 balls.

Important: Unlike previous stages, you do not know which jar the balls are drawn from. You will only see the candidate samples.

You will see {{ C.N_SAMPLES }} candidate samples of {{ C.SAMPLE_SIZE }} balls each. You choose which sample to send to the other player. You will then guess which jar the balls came from.

The other player (Reader)

The other player sees only the sample you choose — they do not know there were other options. They may request up to {{ C.MAX_ADDITIONAL }} additional draws before guessing.

Draw cost: Each additional draw the other player requests costs ${{ draw_cost_display }} and is deducted from their round payoff. This applies in all matches.

You will both guess the jar type. Your payoff depends on both your guesses.

Match structure & draw costs
Match 1 Rounds 1–3 vs the other player Jar 1 — extra draws ${{ draw_cost_display }} each
Match 2 Rounds 4–6 vs the other player Jar 1 — extra draws ${{ draw_cost_display }} each
Match 3 Rounds 7–9 vs the other player Jar 2 (new) — extra draws ${{ draw_cost_display }} each
Match 4 Rounds 10–12 vs the other player Jar 2 — extra draws ${{ draw_cost_display }} each

The jar may change starting in Match 3. Additional draws cost ${{ draw_cost_display }} each in every match.
