Welcome to Stage 6 — Your role: Writer

In this stage you are the Writer. Each round, balls are drawn from either a Red Jar (14 red, 6 blue) or a Blue Jar (14 blue, 6 red) containing 20 balls.

Important: Unlike previous stages, you do not know which jar the balls are drawn from. You will only see the candidate samples.

You will see {{ C.N_SAMPLES }} candidate samples of {{ C.SAMPLE_SIZE }} balls each. You choose which sample to send to your Reader. You will then guess which jar the balls came from.

Your Reader

Your Reader sees only the sample you choose — they do not know there were other options. They may request up to {{ C.MAX_ADDITIONAL }} additional draws before guessing.

Draw cost: Each additional draw your Reader requests costs ${{ draw_cost_display }} and is deducted from their round payoff. This applies in all matches.

You will both guess the jar type. Your payoff depends on both your guesses.

Note: Your Reader may be a real participant or a computer. You will not be told which.
