Welcome to Stage 3

In this stage you will play a coordination game. Each round, you and a partner both look at balls drawn from the same jar and each independently guess whether it is a Red Jar (14 red, 6 blue) or a Blue Jar (14 blue, 6 red).

One jar is used for all 12 rounds. The jar type will not change during Stage 3. You will see 10 balls per round, drawn with replacement.

Match structure
Match 1 Rounds 1–3 vs a partner
Match 2 Rounds 4–6 vs a partner
Match 3 Rounds 7–9 vs a partner
Match 4 Rounds 10–12 vs a partner

You are matched with the same partner for all 3 rounds within a match. Your partner may change between matches.

Note: Each partner may be a real participant or a computer. You will not be told which — you will simply see your partner's choice after each round.
