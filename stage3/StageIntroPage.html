Welcome to Stage 3

Each round, you and the other player both look at balls drawn from the same jar and each independently guess whether it is a Red Jar (14 red, 6 blue) or a Blue Jar (14 blue, 6 red).

The jar may change with each new match. The jar stays the same for the 3 rounds within a match, then may change for the next match. You will see 10 balls per round, drawn with replacement.

Match structure
Match 1 Rounds 1–3 vs the other player
Match 2 Rounds 4–6 vs the other player

You are matched with the same other player for all 3 rounds within a match. The other player may change between matches.
