Stage 1 — Bayesian Learning

In this stage you will observe balls drawn from a jar and try to identify which jar they came from. There are two possible jars:

  • Red Jar: 14 red balls and 6 blue balls
  • Blue Jar: 14 blue balls and 6 red balls

You will play {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} rounds in total, split into two sets of {{ C.ROUNDS_PER_JAR }} rounds:

  • Rounds 1–{{ C.ROUNDS_PER_JAR }}: all draws come from the same jar (Jar 1).
  • Rounds {{ rounds_per_jar_plus_1 }}–{{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }}: all draws come from a new jar (Jar 2), which may be different from Jar 1.

Each round, {{ C.N_DRAWS }} balls are drawn from the jar. You will not be told which jar it is — use the draws to figure it out.

Balls are drawn independently each round (the jar is always full before a draw).
